Snap 24

The 24 game, played in ChatGPT. Deal a hand, combine every card exactly once with + - * /, and land on the target.

What it does

  • Deals Classic hands (five cards, target 24) and Custom hands, across six difficulty tiers.
  • Opens an interactive board — tap two cards and an operator to merge them.
  • Hints one level at a time: which two cards, the operator, the sub-result, then the full solution.
  • Checks a solution with exact fraction arithmetic, so near-misses and reused cards are both caught.
  • Reveals the distinct solutions, and explains one step by step.

Play it

Snap 24 is a ChatGPT plugin. Its MCP endpoint is https://snap24.marcustut.me/mcp (streamable HTTP), and the same server runs over stdio for local tooling.
