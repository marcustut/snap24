Support

Playing Snap 24 with ChatGPT. Most questions are answered below; anything else, get in touch.

How to play

Ask for a hand ("deal me a Snap 24 hand"), then combine every card exactly once with + - * / to reach the target. Tap two cards and an operator on the board to merge them. Ask for a hint if you are stuck, or say your answer out loud to have it checked. A=1, J=11, Q=12, K=13, and fractions are allowed at every step.

Common questions

The cards disappeared mid-game
That is the challenge, not a bug: from Medium upward the hand hides after a few seconds. Higher tiers hide it sooner, and Blind never shows it at all.
"Unknown or expired puzzle id"
A hand lives in memory for 30 minutes, and a server update clears hands too. Ask for a new deal, or say "show me the board again" to bring back the current one.
My answer was rejected even though it equals 24
Every card must be used exactly once. Reusing or skipping a card is rejected, and the reason says which cards were expected.
The board closed or scrolled away
Say "show me the board again" and it comes back with the same hand. On ChatGPT you can also pop the board out so it stays visible while you type.
Why does it say there are hundreds of solutions?
Most hands can be solved many ways. The reveal lists a handful and gives the true total.

Report a problem

Email support@marcustut.me with what you asked for, what you expected, and what happened. Say the word "bug" or "support" so it is not mistaken for a game request. You can also reach the developer at github.com/marcustut.

Snap 24 is a small project: replies usually arrive within a few days.
